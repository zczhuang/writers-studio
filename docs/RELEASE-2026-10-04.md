# Writer's Studio — October 4, 2026

## A new look: midnight ink & gold leaf

- **Night-sky storybook.** Home, rewards, the lock screen, and onboarding now open on an illustrated moonlit landscape with an open book, paper planes, a castle, and a lighthouse. Reading and writing stay on warm paper.
- **Four illustrated worlds.** Each world has its own scene: Lighthouse Coast (Scene), Sunset Road (Story), Moonlit Manor (Mystery), and Word Workshop (Upgrade). They appear on Home and as the header of each chapter.
- **Real medals.** Results reveal a rosette medal in bronze, silver, gold, or platinum, with light rays and confetti for gold and platinum. Badges are gold wax seals; locked ones are pressed paper.
- **A writer's manuscript.** The editor is a ruled page with a margin line, text that sits exactly on the rules, a word-goal ring, and idea cards for the prompt.
- **Navigation.** Desktop gets a sidebar with a "Write a new page" button, rank ring, and save status. Phones get a floating dock. Writing and results stay distraction-free.
- **Type and color.** Fraunces, Figtree, and Literata are self-hosted, so no requests go to Google Fonts and the look works offline. Each of the five skills now has its own color (Vocabulary and Originality were nearly the same gold).
- **Parents.** The dashboard leads with KPIs and a "Saved progress" panel that shows whether progress is on this device only or backed up to the cloud.

## Fixes

- The onboarding "Confirm your PIN" step re-submitted the first PIN automatically, so a parent never actually confirmed it. It now waits for a second entry and catches mismatches.
- A focus outline drew a stray frame around the main content on desktop.
- Money amounts used a font that was never loaded and fell back to Courier.
- Wallet payout rows can be selected with the keyboard, the PIN pad accepts typed digits, and toasts are announced to screen readers.

## Saving progress

- Failed cloud saves retry automatically with backoff, hiding or closing the tab saves immediately, and a device that loses its anonymous sign-in relinks itself with its own recovery code.
- Disabled anonymous sign-ins show a specific "needs one setup step" status.
- No Supabase project is provisioned yet, so production remains device-only until the checklist in [CLOUD-BACKUP.md](CLOUD-BACKUP.md) is completed.

## Validation

- `npm run build`, `npm run lint -- --max-warnings=0`, and `npm test` (108 tests, including 7 new sync and transport tests) pass.
- Browser checks at 320, 390, 768, and 1280 px covered every screen with no horizontal overflow, plus reduced motion, keyboard focus rings, keyboard PIN entry, and the corrected PIN-confirm flow.
